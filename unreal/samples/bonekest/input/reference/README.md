# Visual target guide

These four rendered examples illustrate the numbered GDD visual requirements.
They add no hidden mechanics, fixed maze layout, required room coordinate or exact
pixel-matching rule. Section 6 controls behavior and the final title is BoneMaze.

- 01-chamber.png: VISUAL-01/02/03, MOVE-03 and UI-01. Elevated three-quarter view,
  dark low-poly stone masonry, cyan flames, pale light pools, central robed skull mage,
  book, coarse world image, light corner HUD and coloured key indicators.
- 02-camera-and-floor.png: MOVE-03 and VISUAL-01/02. Tracking composition, floor
  perspective, props, visible walking space and readable character silhouette.
- 03-reveal-spell.png: SPELL-01/02, UI-02 and VISUAL-04/05. A casting pose with ground
  glow/sparks and an explicit monster coordinate readout below the chamber label.
- 04-room-transfer.png: PORTAL-01, VISUAL-01/05 and PICKUP-01. A different chamber
  with scattered gold coins, broken floor slabs and a violet arrival ring. The scene
  illustrates room variety and transition effects, not a required generated layout.

Reference counts/coordinates are incidental states. The required starting values,
reveal duration and exact gameplay rules are stated in the GDD. Images are visual
guidance only, not textures for a fullscreen screenshot-based game.
