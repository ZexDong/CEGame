# Public Asset Rights Boundary

This directory is the public navigation point for asset provenance and use restrictions. It does not grant rights beyond the terms that already apply to the supplied content.

- `ASSET_LICENSE_MAP.json` maps every `license_id` used by `../ASSET_CATALOG.json` to its public notice and restrictions.
- `../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` is the human-readable provenance and terms summary.
- `../ASSET_CATALOG.json` is the per-file authority for exact paths, hashes, provenance, and `license_id` values.

The public package contains Unreal Engine and Epic ecosystem content plus selected assets from the audited ActionRoguelike lineage. Those materials are not relicensed as open-source content by this benchmark. Use them only in an authorized Unreal Engine context, do not export them as a standalone asset pack, and review the applicable Epic terms and upstream authorization before redistribution or commercial use.

No copied license text in this directory should be interpreted as replacing the controlling Epic agreements. The package therefore preserves the official agreement references in `../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` rather than presenting an unofficial translation or modified legal text.
