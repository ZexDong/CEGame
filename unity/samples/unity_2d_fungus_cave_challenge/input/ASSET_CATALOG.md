# Available visual assets
All files listed in ASSET_CATALOG.json are newly authored MIT assets. The contact sheet previews every role and craft silhouette, not a prescribed scene. PNGs are single 96x96 sprites, rect the full image, centered pivot,96PPU, point filtering/no mipmaps, modest transparent margins. OBJ models contain geometry only, nose +Z, no scripts or materials. Assign suitable materials yourself. No prefabs, missing scripts, animation controllers, external fonts, audio recordings or gameplay implementation are supplied. Use an installed Unity font and synthesised sound where required. Role-appropriate primitive terrain, rails and gates may be made in the writable area; do not download art or source solutions.
