# Sources and License Scope

Third-party license files retain their original terms. The root license does not override third-party licenses.
Assets, contracts, and starter projects retain their logical input locations so that relative references remain valid.

- `input/legal/CREDITS.md`
- `input/legal/LICENSE`
- `input/legal/LICENSES.txt`
- `input/legal/NOTICE`

Audio is not uniformly MIT-licensed. Preserve the applicable Stability terms and Powered by Stability AI attribution; project-use distribution terms do not grant unrestricted standalone asset redistribution.
