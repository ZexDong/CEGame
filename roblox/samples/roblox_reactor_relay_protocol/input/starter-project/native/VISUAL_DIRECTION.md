# Public Visual Direction

Create a coherent reactor-control presentation inside the frozen 1600 by 900 logical canvas. The bay-panel and control rectangles are fixed; styling and decoration within the remaining composition are flexible.

- The menu, active board, warnings, preview, sequence input, Won, and Lost states must be visually distinct.
- Bay A, Bay B, and Bay C must remain simultaneously identifiable. Each bay must show current and target power, coolant, phase, stability, lock state, and overload count.
- Use a dark industrial/control-room theme with strong contrast, restrained accent colors, and at least one non-color cue for every critical state.
- Resource, timer, integrity, incident, and terminal information must not cover active controls.
- Buttons must retain readable labels and a minimum visible hit rectangle of 44 by 32 pixels.
- Disabled controls need a visibly different treatment while their labels remain readable.
- Warning feedback should be prominent without hiding required values or controls.
- Decorative 3D elements, lighting, icons, animation, and sound are optional. Debug text, broken placeholders, overlapping controls, unreadable contrast, and hidden required state are prohibited by VISUAL-01.
