# Deep Relay visual direction

Create a readable underground relay facility from Roblox primitives. Use dark structural surfaces, cool cyan navigation and normal-system signals, amber interactions or pending state, red danger/rejection, and green completion. Keep prompts, doors, carry items, safe areas, hazard timing, and final stabilizers visually distinct.

The supplied palette and contact sheet are style ingredients, not a target screenshot or final layout. Room order, coordinates, decoration, and camera composition are not prescribed. Avoid invisible critical objects, overlapping prompts, debug geometry, placeholder labels, and HUD elements that hide the play area.
